When users run our mobile games, we may automatically collect basic device‑related information for service stability analysis, crash reporting, game performance optimization. Our mobile applications may integrate third‑party software development kits (SDKs), including but not limited to statistics SDK, advertising SDK provided by authorized partners. Users may submit inquiries related to personal data via our corporate email, subject to applicable local laws and regulations.
